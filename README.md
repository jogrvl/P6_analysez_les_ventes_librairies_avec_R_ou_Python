# Bookstore Sales & Customer Behavior Analysis – Lapage

## Context
Data Analyst consultant mission for Lapage, a large online bookstore
that launched its e-commerce platform two years prior. The marketing
team needed a comprehensive review of sales performance and customer
behavior to guide strategic decisions.

## Objectives
### Sales Analysis
- Analyze key sales indicators and overall online performance
- Identify strengths, weaknesses and growth opportunities
- Support pricing and promotional strategy decisions

### Customer Behavior Analysis
- Examine the relationship between customer gender and book 
  category preferences
- Analyze correlations between customer age and:
  - Total purchase amount
  - Purchase frequency
  - Average basket size
  - Book category preferences

## Data Sources
- Customer database (demographics, purchase history)
- Online sales transactions (products, categories, amounts)

## Deliverables
- Sales performance report with key indicators
- Statistical correlation analysis on customer behavior
- Jupyter Notebook with documented methodology and visualizations

## Tools & Skills
- Python (pandas, matplotlib, seaborn, scipy)
- Exploratory data analysis (EDA)
- Statistical correlation analysis
- Customer segmentation insights

## Key Learnings
Combining sales analysis with behavioral statistics to deliver
actionable marketing recommendations from multi-table datasets.
